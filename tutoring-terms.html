Free Tutoring Program: Parent Agreement

1. About the program

The Free Tutoring Program ("the Program") is a community service of Faizan e Madina Vancouver (the "Islamic Centre"), a centre of Dawat-e-Islami Canada, a registered Canadian charity (BN 81933 5662 RR0001), located at 7062 134 St, Surrey, BC V3W 4T2. Volunteers give free academic help to students in Grades 2 to 8. The Program is free. It does not replace school, a certified teacher, a registered tutor, or any professional assessment or service.

Requests are not confirmed until the Islamic Centre sends a confirmation by email. Submitting a request places a tentative hold only. The Islamic Centre may decline, reschedule or end any booking or registration at its discretion, including for safety reasons.

2. Consent and program agreement

By registering, I, the parent or legal guardian named in the registration form, confirm and agree that:

  1. I am the parent or legal guardian of the child named in the form and have the legal authority to register the child and give these consents. If another person shares custody or guardianship, I have their agreement where it is legally required.
  2. The information I give is true and complete to the best of my knowledge, and I will tell the Islamic Centre promptly if anything changes, especially health, allergy, custody or emergency contact details.
  3. I give permission for my child to take part in tutoring sessions run by volunteers of the Islamic Centre, in person at the Islamic Centre or online, as confirmed for each booking.
  4. In-person sessions take place only at the Islamic Centre, at 7062 134 St, Surrey, BC V3W 4T2, never at a home, a library or any other place, and never one-to-one in a closed room.
  5. A parent, guardian or adult I authorize in writing will stay on the Islamic Centre premises (for example in the parking lot or a waiting area) for the whole of every in-person session and will be reachable by phone at all times. I will drop off and pick up my child on time. The Islamic Centre may cancel a session if no responsible adult is present.
  6. My child will only leave with me or with an adult I have named in the form as authorized for pick-up.
  7. Volunteers are screened by the Islamic Centre but are not licensed teachers unless they say so. The Islamic Centre does not promise any particular academic result.
  8. The Islamic Centre may limit how often a child can attend (currently one session per month per child, booked no more than one week in advance) and may change these limits.
  9. I may withdraw my child at any time by contacting the Islamic Centre.
  10. The Islamic Centre may end my child's participation if the rules in this agreement are not followed, or if it believes this is necessary for anyone's safety.

3. Rules at the Islamic Centre

The Program takes place in a place of worship. Families of every background are welcome. We ask everyone attending, including parents waiting on the premises, to:

  • Dress modestly and remove shoes where asked.
  • Keep voices low and respect the prayer areas. Sessions may pause briefly during congregational prayer (salah).
  • Be respectful and courteous to worshippers, neighbours and other families in and around the Islamic Centre, including in the parking lot.
  • Not bring food, drinks or items that are not allowed in the Islamic Centre, unless agreed in advance (for example, for medical reasons).
  • Follow the instructions of the Program coordinator and volunteers.

Students are expected to be respectful, follow instructions and stay in the tutoring area. No one may take photos, video or audio recordings of other students or volunteers.

4. Online session rules

  • Online sessions use the Islamic Centre's own account and a link sent by the Islamic Centre. Volunteers will not use personal accounts, personal phone numbers or private messaging with students.
  • A parent or guardian must be in the same room, or within sight and hearing, for the whole session.
  • The student should join from a shared space in the home (not a bedroom with the door closed), with the camera on where possible.
  • Neither the family nor the volunteer may record online sessions unless the Islamic Centre confirms in writing that a session will be recorded and why.
  • All communication about sessions goes through the Islamic Centre's email or phone, and is copied to the parent.

5. Camera (video recording) notice

The Islamic Centre uses video cameras in its building and grounds, including the area used for tutoring, to protect students, volunteers and visitors, and to provide a record if a concern or incident is raised.

  • Cameras record video and may record sound. Signs are posted at the entrances.
  • Footage is kept for a short fixed period (currently about 30 days) and is then automatically overwritten, unless it is needed to look into an incident, concern or legal claim, in which case it is kept securely for as long as needed.
  • Footage is viewed only by people authorized by the Islamic Centre, and is only shared outside the Islamic Centre with police or other authorities where required or permitted by law, or with legal advisers and insurers.
  • The Islamic Centre does not post photos or videos of children in the Program on social media or its website.

By registering, I consent to my child being recorded by these cameras while attending the Program. If I do not agree, my child may take part in online sessions only.

6. Health, allergies and emergencies

  • I have listed all known allergies, medical conditions, medications and other needs the Islamic Centre should know about, and will update them if they change.
  • If my child has a prescribed emergency medication (for example an epinephrine auto-injector or inhaler), my child will bring it to every session, and I will be on the premises to give it or help. Volunteers are not expected to give medication except in a life-threatening emergency.
  • In an emergency, I authorize the Islamic Centre to call 911, to give basic first aid, and to share my child's health and emergency information with paramedics and medical staff. The Islamic Centre will try to contact me first and then my emergency contacts.
  • I will keep my child at home if they are unwell or have a contagious illness.

7. Acknowledgement of risk and release

I understand that attending any program, including travel to and from the Islamic Centre and time on its premises, carries some risk of illness, injury, loss or damage to belongings. The Islamic Centre takes reasonable steps to keep students safe, as described in this agreement.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I, on my own behalf, release the Islamic Centre, Dawat-e-Islami Canada, and their directors, staff and volunteers from claims I may personally have for loss, damage or injury arising from my child's participation in the Program, except where caused by their negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of law. Nothing in this agreement limits any right my child has under the law, or any duty the Islamic Centre owes to my child.

I understand the Islamic Centre is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged personal belongings.

8. Privacy notice

The Islamic Centre collects personal information under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). We collect only what we need to run the Program safely.

What we collect
Parent and child names and contact details; the child's date of birth, grade and school; learning needs and photos of schoolwork you choose to send; allergies, medical and emergency information; preferences you give us (such as tutor preference, and gender or pronouns, which are optional); your consents and electronic signature; booking history; and camera footage while on the premises.
Why we collect it
To review and confirm requests, match students with suitable volunteers, plan sessions, keep students safe, respond to emergencies, keep a record of consents and attendance, and protect students, volunteers and the Islamic Centre if a concern or claim is raised.
Who can see it
Only the Program coordinator and a small number of people authorized by the Islamic Centre. The volunteer assigned to your child sees only what they need (for example first name, grade, learning needs and allergies). We do not sell or share your information, except with emergency services, police or other authorities where required or permitted by law, or with our legal advisers and insurers.
Where it is stored
In secure online accounts owned by the Islamic Centre (Google services), which may store data on servers outside Canada. Access is protected by passwords and two-step verification.
How long we keep it
Photos of schoolwork and session notes: deleted within about 4 months. Booking records: about 2 years. Signed consents, attendance and any incident records: kept until at least the child's 21st birthday, or longer if needed for a legal claim. Camera footage: about 30 days unless needed for an incident.
Your choices
You may ask to see or correct the information we hold, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. Withdrawing consent may mean your child can no longer take part. Some records (such as signed consents and incident records) may need to be kept for legal reasons.
Contact
Privacy contact, Faizan e Madina Vancouver, 7062 134 St, Surrey, BC V3W 4T2, phone +1 (778) 895-0886. Email: privacy email TBC

9. Signing electronically

I agree that typing my full legal name and ticking the agreement boxes in the registration form is my electronic signature, and has the same effect as signing a paper copy. The Islamic Centre will keep a record of the date and time of my agreement and the version of this agreement I accepted. I may ask for a copy at any time.
