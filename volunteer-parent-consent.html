2. Permission

I am the parent or legal guardian of the volunteer named above and have the legal authority to sign this form. I give permission for my child to volunteer with Faizan e Madina Vancouver (the "Islamic Centre") at the Islamic Centre and at other event locations it organizes, such as rented halls, as confirmed in the shift emails. I understand that volunteering is unpaid, voluntary, and not employment, and that the Islamic Centre may decline, change or end any volunteer role at any time.

3. Tasks and safety

  • Volunteers may be asked to help with tasks such as setting up and moving chairs, tables and boxes, greeting guests, parking, cleaning, serving food and helping at stands. My child may refuse any task at any time.
  • Volunteers under 18 will not be asked to cook, use ladders, direct traffic on public roads, handle cleaning chemicals, do trades work (electrical, plumbing, roofing, pest control), or drive other people.
  • My child will follow the instructions of the volunteer supervisors, the volunteer code of conduct, and the rules of the Islamic Centre, including the separate arrangements for brothers and sisters.
  • I have told the Islamic Centre about any health condition that affects my child's ability to volunteer safely, and I am responsible for my child's travel to and from the event location unless I arrange otherwise in writing.

4. Medical emergencies

In an emergency, I authorize the Islamic Centre to call 911, to give basic first aid, and to share my child's health and contact information with paramedics and medical staff. The Islamic Centre will try to contact me first.

5. Photos and video

Brothers: male volunteers may be photographed and filmed while volunteering, and these photos and videos may be used on the Islamic Centre's website and social media. Agreeing to this is a condition of volunteering for brothers.

Sisters: sister volunteers are never photographed or filmed.

6. Acknowledgement of risk and release

I understand that volunteering, including physical tasks, working at events and travel to and from event locations, carries some risk of injury, illness, or loss or damage to belongings. The Islamic Centre takes reasonable steps to keep volunteers safe. To the fullest extent permitted by law, I, on my own behalf, release Faizan e Madina Vancouver, Dawat-e-Islami Canada, and their directors, staff and volunteers from claims I may personally have for loss, damage or injury arising from my child's volunteering, except where caused by their negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of law. Nothing in this form limits any right my child has under the law, or any duty the Islamic Centre owes to my child.

7. Privacy

The Islamic Centre collects the information on this form and on my child's online application under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, to organize volunteers safely, contact us in an emergency, record attendance and hours, and write volunteer hours letters. It is seen only by authorized volunteer supervisors and is kept as described in the volunteer agreement.

8. Signatures

I have read this form and the volunteer agreement (available at dawateislamivancouver.ca/volunteer-terms.html), I understand them, and I agree.
