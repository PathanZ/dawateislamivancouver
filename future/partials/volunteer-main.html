Sisters, we need you!

Sister volunteers serve in the sisters' areas, separate from the brothers, and are never photographed or filmed. Your safety and comfort come first.

  1. 1
    Apply once

    Tell us about your skills and how you'd like to help. It takes about 7 minutes.

  2. 2
    We review

    A volunteer supervisor reviews your application and approves the jobs that suit you.

  3. 3
    Get your shifts

    Choose shifts at upcoming events, or we'll assign you. We email a confirmation and a reminder the day before.

  4. 4
    Earn your hours

    Your hours are recorded. Students can request a volunteer hours letter signed by the Imam.
