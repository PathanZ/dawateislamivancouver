Why give

Your donation keeps our Islamic Centre going

✦

Donations support Faizan e Madina Vancouver: the Islamic Centre, Qur'an classes, the weekend school and community programs. Zakat, Sadaqah and Fitra are all accepted, along with sponsorship of scholars' education, as are donations toward the Islamic Centre's mortgage and Qarz-e-Hasna contributions.

"Charity does not decrease wealth." Sahih Muslim, 2588

Every donation receives an official tax receipt

Donations to Faizan e Madina Vancouver receive an official donation receipt for income tax purposes, which you can use to claim a charitable tax credit with the CRA. Registered Canadian charity, BN 81933 5662 RR0001.

Qarz-e-Hasna calculator

Qarz-e-Hasna is an interest-free loan to the Islamic Centre that is paid back to you. Each dollar you lend replaces a dollar of bank loan, so we stop paying interest on it.

Fully documented and official. Every Qarz-e-Hasna loan is recorded in a written, notarized loan agreement signed by both sides. It's not just a handshake: it's a legally binding document that can be enforced in court.
