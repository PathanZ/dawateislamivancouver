Map style options

Five looks for the Canada map on the home page. Each one animates when you scroll to it. Tell us which number you like.

Option 1: Emerald (currently live)

Solid dark-green Canada in the website's colours, gold pins and gold lines from Vancouver.

Option 2: Light and gold

A light, airy map: cream Canada with a green outline, green pins and gold lines. Calm and classic.

Option 3: Night glow

A dark night-sky look. Glowing gold pins and lines stand out like lights across Canada.

Option 4: Dotted

A modern dotted map. Canada is drawn with small green dots that ripple in from west to east.

Option 5: Province colours

Each province in a different shade of green, so people can easily see which province each centre is in.
