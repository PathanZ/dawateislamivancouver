1. About volunteering with us

Faizan e Madina Vancouver (the "Islamic Centre") is a centre of Dawat-e-Islami Canada, a registered Canadian charity (BN 81933 5662 RR0001), at 7062 134 St, Surrey, BC V3W 4T2. Volunteers help with events, classes and the everyday running of the Islamic Centre. Volunteering is unpaid and freely chosen. It is not employment, and you may stop at any time.

Volunteers must be at least 16. Every application is reviewed by a volunteer supervisor, who decides which jobs each volunteer is approved for. The Islamic Centre may accept, decline, move or end any application, role or shift, including for safety reasons. A shift is only booked when you receive our confirmation email.

Events may take place at the Islamic Centre or at other locations, such as rented halls. The location of each shift is given in your confirmation and reminder emails.

Brothers and sisters volunteer in separate areas, with separate roles, following the Islamic Centre's arrangements.

2. Choosing your tasks safely

  • You choose the tasks you do. You may say no to any task at any time, without giving a reason, and no one will pressure you.
  • Moving chairs, tables, equipment and boxes is the most needed task at our events. You agree to tell us honestly on your application, and straight away if anything changes, whether you can lift and move items of about 10 to 20 kg. If you cannot, we may only be able to offer limited roles, such as greeting guests.
  • Only do physical tasks you can do safely. Ask for help with anything heavy, and lift with a partner when in doubt.
  • Trades work (electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, pest control) is only done by volunteers who hold the licence or certification required in British Columbia, and only when a supervisor asks.
  • Volunteers who help with transport or moving must hold a valid driver's licence of the right class for the vehicle, and use an insured vehicle. Trucks, vans and trailers are only used when a supervisor asks, and loads must be properly secured.
  • Follow the instructions of the event coordinator and use any safety equipment provided (for example gloves in the kitchen or high-visibility vests in the parking lot).
  • Do not use ladders, kitchen equipment, cleaning chemicals or tools unless you have been shown how and are allowed to.
  • Volunteers under 18 do not cook, use ladders, direct traffic on public roads, handle cleaning chemicals, do trades work, or drive other people.

3. Code of conduct

  • Treat everyone with respect and kindness: guests, worshippers, neighbours and other volunteers. No harassment, bullying, discrimination or abusive language.
  • Dress modestly, keep voices low near the prayer areas, and respect the brothers' and sisters' arrangements.
  • Arrive on time for your shift and check in with the supervisor. If you cannot come, you must cancel using the link in your email and call a volunteer supervisor as early as possible.
  • Do not come under the influence of alcohol, cannabis or other drugs. No smoking or vaping on the premises.
  • Keep any personal information you see (such as names, phone numbers or health details) private.
  • Do not take money on behalf of the Islamic Centre unless you are asked to by the coordinator, and hand over all donations received immediately.
  • Report any injury, incident, safety concern or complaint to the coordinator the same day.

4. Working with children

Roles with children, such as tutoring and kids' activities, require a current Vulnerable Sector Check (a criminal record check for working with children), arranged by the Islamic Centre, before you start. In these roles you agree that:

  • You will never be alone with a child out of sight of other adults.
  • You will not contact children directly by phone, text or social media, and will not take photos of them.
  • You will not give children rides or meet them outside Islamic Centre activities.
  • If a child tells you they are being harmed, or you believe a child needs protection, you will report it as BC law requires, by calling the Helpline for Children at 310-1234 (or 911 if the child is in immediate danger), and tell the coordinator.

5. Health, safety and emergencies

  • Please stay home if you are unwell.
  • In an emergency, the Islamic Centre may call 911, give basic first aid, and contact your emergency contact.
  • You do not have to tell us about any health condition. If something would help you volunteer comfortably, you may tell the coordinator.

6. Acknowledgement of risk and release

I understand that volunteering, including physical tasks, working in kitchens and parking areas, and travel to and from the Islamic Centre, carries some risk of injury, illness, or loss or damage to belongings. The Islamic Centre takes reasonable steps to keep volunteers safe, as described in this agreement. I accept the risks of the tasks I choose to do.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release the Islamic Centre, Dawat-e-Islami Canada, and their directors, staff and volunteers from claims for loss, damage or injury arising from my volunteering, except where caused by their negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of law. The Islamic Centre is not responsible for lost or stolen personal belongings.

7. Volunteers under 18

Volunteers aged 16 and 17 must confirm on the application that their parent or guardian agrees, and must bring the Parent/Guardian Consent Form, signed, to their first shift. They cannot start volunteering without it. Nothing in this agreement limits any right a person under 18 has under the law, or any duty the Islamic Centre owes to them.

8. Photos

Brothers: photos and videos are taken at our events. As a condition of volunteering, male volunteers agree to be photographed and filmed while volunteering, and for these photos and videos to be used on the Islamic Centre's website and social media. For volunteers under 18, the parent or guardian gives this permission on the consent form. If you have a personal safety reason not to be photographed, please speak to a supervisor before your shift.

Sisters: sister volunteers are never photographed or filmed.

9. Volunteer hours letters

The coordinator records the shifts you attend. Students may request a letter confirming their verified hours. Each letter is reviewed and approved by the Imam before it is sent, and only includes hours we have recorded. Letters are sent only to the email you signed up with.

10. Privacy notice

The Islamic Centre collects personal information under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). We collect only what we need to organize volunteers safely.

What we collect
Your name, phone, email and city; your age group; whether you are a brother or sister; two emergency contacts; your education, experience, certificates and their expiry dates, and police check type and date; whether you can lift and move equipment; the areas you'd like to help with, and any equipment, software or trade licence you tell us about; whether you have an insured car, pickup truck, van or trailer, and your driver's licence class (brothers); your availability; for students, your school and grade; for volunteers under 18, a parent or guardian's details and consent form; your shifts; attendance records kept by supervisors (check-in and check-out times, work done and short notes); and your agreement and electronic signature.
Why we collect it
To review applications, match volunteers to the right tasks, plan shifts, send confirmations and reminders, keep everyone safe, contact someone in an emergency, arrange the brothers' and sisters' areas, keep a record of agreements and attendance, and write volunteer hours letters. Supervisor notes are kept factual and respectful, and you may ask to see them.
What we don't ask
We do not ask about your health, disabilities, religion, ethnicity, marital status, immigration status, social insurance number or criminal history on this form.
Who can see it
Volunteer supervisors see what they need to organize events (your name, approved jobs, availability and phone). Emergency contacts, age, parent details and police check information are seen only by one or two volunteer coordinators. We do not sell or share your information, except with emergency services or authorities where required or permitted by law, or with our legal advisers and insurers.
Where it is stored
In secure online accounts owned by the Islamic Centre (Google services), which may store data on servers outside Canada. Access is protected by passwords and two-step verification.
How long we keep it
Your volunteer profile is kept while you volunteer with us and deleted after 2 years without activity. Signed agreements, attendance records and any incident records are kept for at least 7 years, or until a volunteer under 18 turns 21, whichever is later.
Your choices
You may ask to see or correct your information, or to be removed from our volunteer list, by contacting us.

11. Signing electronically

I agree that typing my full name and ticking the agreement boxes on the sign-up form is my electronic signature, and has the same effect as signing a paper copy. The Islamic Centre keeps a record of the date and time and the version of this agreement I accepted. Volunteers renew this agreement once a year. I may ask for a copy at any time.
