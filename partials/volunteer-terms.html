1. About volunteering with us

%%V_NAME%% (the "Islamic Centre") is a centre of Dawat-e-Islami Canada, a registered Canadian charity (BN %%BN%%), at %%V_ADDRESS%%. Volunteers help with events, classes and the everyday running of the Islamic Centre. Volunteering is unpaid and freely chosen. It is not employment, and you may stop at any time.

The Islamic Centre may accept, decline, move or end any volunteer sign-up or shift, including for safety reasons. A shift is only booked when you receive our confirmation email. Volunteers aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's approval.

2. Choosing your tasks safely

  • You choose the tasks you do. You may say no to any task at any time, without giving a reason, and no one will pressure you.
  • Some tasks involve physical work, such as lifting or carrying chairs, tables, boxes or food containers, standing for long periods, or working outdoors in parking areas. Only do these if you can do them safely. Ask for help with anything heavy, and lift with a partner when in doubt.
  • Follow the instructions of the event coordinator and use any safety equipment provided (for example gloves in the kitchen or high-visibility vests in the parking lot).
  • Do not use ladders, kitchen equipment, cleaning chemicals or tools unless you have been shown how and are allowed to.
  • Volunteers under 18 do not do kitchen cooking, ladder work, traffic control on the street, or handle cleaning chemicals.

3. Code of conduct

  • Treat everyone with respect and kindness: guests, worshippers, neighbours and other volunteers. No harassment, bullying, discrimination or abusive language.
  • Dress modestly, keep voices low near the prayer areas, and respect the brothers' and sisters' arrangements.
  • Arrive on time for your shift. If you cannot come, cancel using the link in your email or tell the coordinator as early as possible.
  • Do not come under the influence of alcohol, cannabis or other drugs. No smoking or vaping on the premises.
  • Keep any personal information you see (such as names, phone numbers or health details) private.
  • Do not take money on behalf of the Islamic Centre unless you are asked to by the coordinator, and hand over all donations received immediately.
  • Report any injury, incident, safety concern or complaint to the coordinator the same day.

4. Working with children

Roles with children, such as tutoring and kids' activities, require a criminal record check with a vulnerable sector search, arranged and paid for by the Islamic Centre, before you start. In these roles you agree that:

  • You will never be alone with a child out of sight of other adults.
  • You will not contact children directly by phone, text or social media, and will not take photos of them.
  • You will not give children rides or meet them outside Islamic Centre activities.
  • If a child tells you they are being harmed, or you believe a child needs protection, you will report it as BC law requires, by calling the Helpline for Children at 310-1234 (or 911 if the child is in immediate danger), and tell the coordinator.

5. Health, safety and emergencies

  • Please stay home if you are unwell.
  • In an emergency, the Islamic Centre may call 911, give basic first aid, and contact your emergency contact.
  • You do not have to tell us about any health condition. If something would help you volunteer comfortably, you may tell the coordinator.

6. Acknowledgement of risk and release

I understand that volunteering, including physical tasks, working in kitchens and parking areas, and travel to and from the Islamic Centre, carries some risk of injury, illness, or loss or damage to belongings. The Islamic Centre takes reasonable steps to keep volunteers safe, as described in this agreement. I accept the risks of the tasks I choose to do.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release the Islamic Centre, Dawat-e-Islami Canada, and their directors, staff and volunteers from claims for loss, damage or injury arising from my volunteering, except where caused by their negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of law. The Islamic Centre is not responsible for lost or stolen personal belongings.

7. Volunteers under 18

If you are under 18, your parent or guardian receives an email with this agreement and must approve before your shifts are booked. By approving, the parent or guardian confirms they have read this agreement, give permission for you to volunteer, and agree to it on their own behalf. Nothing in this agreement limits any right a person under 18 has under the law, or any duty the Islamic Centre owes to them.

8. Photos

Photos may be taken at events. We will only use photos where you can be recognized on our website or social media if you said yes on the sign-up form. You can change your mind at any time by contacting us. We never post photos of volunteers under 18 without their parent or guardian's consent.

9. Volunteer hours letters

The coordinator records the shifts you attend. Students may request a letter confirming their verified hours. Each letter is reviewed and approved by the Imam before it is sent, and only includes hours we have recorded. Letters are sent only to the email you signed up with.

10. Privacy notice

The Islamic Centre collects personal information under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). We collect only what we need to organize volunteers safely.

What we collect
Your name, phone, email and city; your age group; your gender if you choose to give it; your emergency contact; your education, experience, certificates and languages; the areas you'd like to help with; for students, your school and grade; for volunteers under 18, a parent or guardian's name and contact details; whether you're willing to get a police check (for roles with children only); your shift sign-ups and attendance; your photo choice; and your agreement and electronic signature.
Why we collect it
To match volunteers to the right tasks, plan shifts, send confirmations and reminders, keep everyone safe, contact someone in an emergency, arrange the brothers' and sisters' areas, keep a record of agreements and attendance, and write volunteer hours letters.
What we don't ask
We do not ask about your health, disabilities, religion, ethnicity, marital status, immigration status, social insurance number or criminal history on this form.
Who can see it
Only the volunteer coordinators and a small number of people authorized by the Islamic Centre. We do not sell or share your information, except with emergency services or authorities where required or permitted by law, or with our legal advisers and insurers.
Where it is stored
In secure online accounts owned by the Islamic Centre (Google services), which may store data on servers outside Canada. Access is protected by passwords and two-step verification.
How long we keep it
Your volunteer profile is kept while you volunteer with us and deleted after 2 years without activity. Signed agreements, attendance records and any incident records are kept for at least 7 years, or until a volunteer under 18 turns 21, whichever is later.
Your choices
You may ask to see or correct your information, or to be removed from our volunteer list, by contacting us.
