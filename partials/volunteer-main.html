Volunteer with us

Give a few hours at %%V_NAME%%: help at big events, in the kitchen, with parking, teaching and more. Students can earn a signed volunteer hours letter.

  1. 1
    Sign up once

    Tell us about your skills and how you'd like to help. It takes about 5 minutes.

  2. 2
    Pick your shifts

    Choose the times that suit you at upcoming events. Each shift shows how many helpers are still needed.

  3. 3
    Get a reminder

    We email you a confirmation right away and a reminder the day before, with a link if you need to cancel.

  4. 4
    Earn your hours

    Your hours are recorded. Students can request a volunteer hours letter signed by the Imam.
