    About us

    Part of the global family of Dawat-e-Islami

    ✦

    Dawat-e-Islami was founded in 1981 in Karachi by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri. Today it serves communities around the world through Qur'an education, Islamic scholarship and welfare work.

    Faizan e Madina Vancouver is Dawat-e-Islami's centre for the Vancouver area, one of several Faizan e Madina centres across Canada.
