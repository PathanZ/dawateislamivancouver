Free Tutoring for Grades 2 to 8

Academic help from Faizan e Madina volunteers, for families in Newton and across Surrey

Community services

Serving our neighbours in Newton–Surrey

Many families in our area are looking for affordable help with schoolwork. Volunteers from Faizan e Madina Vancouver sit down with students and give free, patient support in reading, writing, math and more, in person at the Islamic Centre or online.

The program is open to all families in the community, of any faith or background.

  • WhoStudents in Grades 2 to 8
  • WhereAt 7062 134 St, Surrey, BC V3W 4T2, or online
  • CostFree
  • How oftenOne session per child each month, booked up to one week ahead

How it works

  1. Register and request a date. Tell us about your child and what they need help with. Choose a session in the next 7 days.
  2. Your date is held for you. Your request is marked as pending while we find a suitable volunteer.
  3. We confirm by email. Your session is only booked once you receive our confirmation.
  4. Attend the session. A parent or guardian stays on the premises, or in the room for online sessions.

Keeping every child safe

Our safety promise

Our safety rules apply to every session.

Screened volunteers

Every tutor has a police vulnerable sector check and agrees to our code of conduct.

Never one-to-one alone

Sessions take place in an open, visible area with more than one adult present. Never in a closed room.

Parents nearby

A parent or guardian stays on the premises for in-person sessions, or in the room for online ones.

Cameras on site

The tutoring area is recorded on video for everyone's protection. Footage is kept private.
